## E1. The Verified-Statement-Marketplace (beyond "Translation Proof")
- **What:** verification becomes an economic good — a claim's **Certification Weight** (compounding:
  verifier-kill-rate × consensus × downstream-load × time-signed) is the unit of value.
- **Our proof:** `lib/certificate.py` (CW 36→1683 over 10yr) + `experiment-signed-statement.py`
  (sign+verify+tamper-detect). PROVEN.
- **Why it matters:** as AI floods content, *verified* statements become the scarce asset. This is the
  moat that compounds, not just a product.

## E2. The Co-Evolving Epistemic Organism (beyond "Education")
- **What:** learners aren't consumers — they're a distributed sensor network. Their confusions reveal
  ambiguity in the SOURCE, driving source-repair that improves the scholarship AND the teaching.
- **Our proof:** `lib/organism.py` + `lib/organism_loop.py` + `lib/pedagogy.py` (8/8 + 7/7 + 10/10).
  The consumer→research→repair→re-teach loop is validated.
- **Why:** the misconception graph is unscrapeable + self-improving — the rarest moat.

## E3. The What-If Machine (beyond "Research Packet")
