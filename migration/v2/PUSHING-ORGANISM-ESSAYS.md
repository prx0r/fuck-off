# PUSHING · ORGANISM · ESSAYS — the human-curiosity → scholarship-machine layer

*2026-08-14 · Three bodies of work were NOT surfaced in the RECONCILIATION/PRODUCTS/EXPANSIONS docs.
This doc gives them full visibility in the handover. They form the **human side** of the Verified
Epistemic OS: live human questioning (LOGICVID gold) → the organism (consumers as probes) → essays
(scholarship as machine-readable, essay-derived objects). All PROVEN as mechanisms in the lab.*

---

## 1. THE LOGICVID / PUSHING GOLD (live human curiosity as prima materia)

**What it is:** the author's actual live scholarly questioning (SPEC-40..48 + the tantraloka sessions)
— NOT synthetic. The rarest data: real examples of what a curious human finds interesting and why.

**What analyzing it PROVED:**
- `experiment-curiosity-patterns.py` — curiosity is NOT random; it has repeatable structure:
  live-issue (does X explain or rename?), distinction-forensics, tension, honest-boundary.
- `experiment-enquiry-discovery.py` — an enquiry REVEALS topic structure (taxonomy + theorem +
  boundary + frontier), not just questions. The presence enquiry proved prakāśa≠presence≠experience≠
  consciousness.
- `experiment-question-growth.py` — questions are a growth graph; the same primitive reached from many
  directions = robust.
- `experiment-gem-extraction.py` — agentic pushing extracts UNSEEN gems (PENETRATION 1: the text
  asserts a collapse it doesn't prove).

**For the migration:** the LOGICVID gold is the **training signal for question-generation** — the target
the Question-Growth Engine must learn to produce. It's also the **pedagogical structure** (learners
reconstruct the discovered structure).

**Vision:** `VISION-QUESTION-GROWTH-ENGINE.md` + `VISION-ENQUIRY-DISCOVERY-ORGANISM.md`.
**Gold registry:** `../docs/LOGICVID-GOLD-EXEMPLARS.md`.

## 2. THE ORGANISM / CONSUMERS (consumers as probes, not consumers)

**What it is:** every consumer is a probe into the graph. Their questions reveal what's missing, their
objections form a distributed adversarial-review system, their confusions reveal source ambiguity.

**What it PROVED:**
- `lib/organism.py` + `lib/organism_loop.py` — the consumer→research machine (probe→gap→intervention→
  proposal→human-gate), 8/8.
- `lib/pedagogy.py` — adaptive pedagogy driven by what the learner cannot do, 7/7.
- `lib/agent_delivery.py` — clean, safe agent delivery (contracts, context-routing, budget, human gate).

**For the migration:** the organism is how the lab becomes **self-improving from real user data** — the
unscrapeable moat. Consumers probe → gaps → source-repair → better scholarship → sharper probes.

**Vision:** `VISION-COEVOLVING-EPISTEMIC-ORGANISM.md` + `SPEC-20-EDUCATION-ORGANISM.md`.

## 3. THE ESSAYS (scholarship as machine, not just prose)

**What exists (real data):** research-library has a **scholarly commentary set** — Ratié literature
review, the 7-step quote-analysis protocol, convergence nodes with graded claims (Ratié exact vs Solms
vs reconstruction), master tensions, and dozens of essays (IPVV vs Tantrāloka, spanda, buddhist,
soteriology...).

**The essay mechanism (this is the expansion):**
- **essays are not endpoints — they're object-derivation engines.** Each scholarly essay (Ratié's) is a
  dense bundle of: claims, source citations, argument moves, tensions, and graded interpretations. The
  machine should mine essays to DERIVE canonical objects, not just read them.
- **Ratié as the Stage-1 engine** (already noted in `COMMENTARY-STAGE1-ratie-engine.md`): her essays
  provide the reflexive/manifest/free analysis that grounds the argument layer.

**Why it's insane as a mechanism:** hundreds of scholar essays (Ratié, Torella, Dyczkowski, Solms) on
Tantra are currently *prose*. Our verification/review/synthesis machinery can turn them into:
- **claim objects** (with source citations → the epistemic envelope)
- **argument graphs** (the AIF structure in the prose)
- **cruxes** (where scholars genuinely disagree — the master tension Ratié vs Solms surfaced)
- **review packets** (essay claims reviewed against the primary text)
- **comparison units** (cross-scholar: does Ratié's camatkāra equal Solms' pleasure?)

The essay becomes a **derivation input**, not a dead artifact.

**Proofs to build:** `experiment-essay-as-engine.py` (mine an essay → claim + argument + crux objects).
