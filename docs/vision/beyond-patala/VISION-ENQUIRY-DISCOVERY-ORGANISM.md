# VISION — THE ENQUIRY-AS-DISCOVERY ORGANISM (questions that reveal topic structure)

*2026-08-14. Prima materia: the LOGICVID gold exemplars (SPEC-40..48) — the author's live human
questioning, especially the presence enquiry (SPEC-46). The key realization: **the questioning is not
just curiosity — it's DATA ABOUT THE TOPIC ITSELF.** An enquiry doesn't satisfy a questioner; it
REVEALS topic structure (taxonomy + theorem + boundary + frontier). This vision makes that discovery
mechanism the engine of our organism, pedagogy graph, and research machine.*

---

## THE PRIMA MATERIA (what the presence enquiry proved)

The enquiry into "presence vs consciousness" **discovered** (not just asked):

```text
TAXONOMY   prakāśa → presence → experience → consciousness (3 distinct ontologies)
THEOREM    "Reality is manifest before it is interpreted as belonging to a subject."
BOUNDARY   has NOT proved: one Self · Śiva · universal consciousness
FRONTIER   "What turns mere presence into conscious presence?" (why prakāśa always has vimarśa)
```

The enquiry revealed the domain's internal structure. That structure is simultaneously:
- **ontology data** (new concept distinctions — prakāśa ≠ presence ≠ experience ≠ consciousness)
- **research signal** (the boundary = a high-value research gap)
- **pedagogy progression** (the learner should reconstruct this exact structure)
- **a new question-root** (the frontier → the Question-Growth tree continues)

## THE NOVEL MECHANISM: "DISCOVERY PROGRESSION"

The enquiry output is a **progression** that both reveals structure AND teaches it:

```text
DiscoveryProgression = [taxonomy steps...] → theorem → boundary → frontier
```

- **For research:** the boundary and frontier are the growth signal — "what's not established + where
  fields diverge" = the highest-value open questions.
- **For pedagogy:** the progression IS the curriculum — the learner reconstructs each step, and a
  learner's confusion at a step is the organism's demand signal (feeds repair cascade).
- **For ontology:** the taxonomy is a distinction-graph — new concept nodes/edges (GraphProposal →
  human gate).

This is the deep link: **the same structure that organizes the topic is what the learner must
reconstruct and what the researcher must attack.** One structure, three uses — the "one graph becomes
scholarship, benchmark, education, assessment, tutoring and media" principle, now with questions as
the growth force.
